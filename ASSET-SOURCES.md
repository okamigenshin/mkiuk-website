# Website asset sources

The MKI logo, showroom photograph and project composite were recovered from MKI's own archived website. The original logo asset is retained at `assets/mki-logo.webp`; the footer uses CSS inversion for contrast. The curtain photograph is style inspiration and is not presented as an MKI installation. See README.md for the archive and recovery limits.

The following original logo bytes were retrieved from image references on the brands' official websites on 11 October 2026. Logos identify the brands under discussion; they do not establish a dealer relationship. Luxaflex is the verified exception, supported by its official Martin Kaye Interiors dealer listing.

The current Luxaflex mark includes the manufacturer's 75-year lock-up. The assets are displayed without redrawing or recolouring. SVG safety checks reject scripts, embedded images, event handlers and external references. Original SHA-256 hashes and dimensions are retained in the ignored local provenance record.

Designers Guild's official site required browser verification, and no verified downloadable logo was obtained. Somfy's official asset request was blocked and its live page could not be inspected reliably for another mark. Both remain text references linking to their official sites.

`assets/window-shutters.svg`, `assets/motorised-blinds.svg` and `assets/blind-styles.svg` are original explanatory illustrations. They are labelled as illustrations and do not depict actual products, exact specifications or completed customer work.
