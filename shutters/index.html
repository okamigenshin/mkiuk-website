Made-to-measure shutters in Mill Hill

Plan privacy, daylight and a fit that works with your windows.

Choose the right panel layout

Full-height shutters cover the full opening. Café-style shutters cover the lower part, leaving the top open, while tier-on-tier layouts allow upper and lower panels to operate separately. Discuss which approach suits your privacy, daylight and window access needs.

Panel widths, louvre size and how the shutters fold back affect the finished layout. A bay window needs particular attention to the angles, handles and clearances between adjoining panels.

Materials and room conditions

Compare the finish, weight and maintenance requirements of timber, engineered materials and suitable moisture-resistant options. The right choice depends on the specific product and the room, especially in bathrooms or areas exposed to condensation.

Ask about available finishes and manufacturer care instructions. We can discuss the specification for your space before you choose a product.

A fit that keeps the window usable

The survey should account for recess depth, frame position, opening direction, handles and any vents or obstructions. Measuring only the width and height is not enough to establish the final fit.

Contact our Mill Hill studio to discuss your windows and arrange the next step. Material, style and product availability are confirmed as part of the enquiry.

Common questions

What is the difference between café-style and full-height shutters?

Café-style shutters cover the lower portion of the window. Full-height shutters cover the full opening. The choice depends on where you need privacy and how you want to control daylight.

What should I consider for bathroom shutters?

Discuss moisture exposure, ventilation and the manufacturer’s suitability guidance for the chosen material. Confirm that the proposed product is suitable for your room before ordering.
