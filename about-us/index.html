About Martin Kaye Interiors

Bespoke curtains, blinds and soft furnishings since 1995.

Our curtain, blind and soft furnishing showroom is in Mill Hill, North London. Seeing the samples and finished treatments in person helps clients choose what will work best for their windows.

We visit site to measure, advise and plan the right treatment. Curtains, blinds, upholstery and feature walls are then made in our in-house workshop and installed by our fitting team.

Our work spans private homes and commercial spaces, including restaurants and hotels. We also work with interior designers, architects and builders.
