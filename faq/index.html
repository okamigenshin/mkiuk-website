Frequently asked questions

Practical answers before you choose your window treatments or visit our showroom.

What are your showroom opening hours?

Our showroom is open Monday–Friday, 08:30–17:30. Contact the team to arrange a suitable time for your visit.

Where is the Martin Kaye Interiors showroom?

Our showroom is at 625 Watford Way, Mill Hill, London, NW7 3JN. Call 020 8906 9900 or email info@mkiuk.com to arrange a visit.

What does Martin Kaye Interiors make?

We make bespoke curtains, blinds and soft furnishings, with design and fitting support. You can also discuss shutters, motorised blinds, upholstery and feature walls with our team.

Do you work with interior designers and hotels?

Yes. We work with interior designers, hotels and private clients. Tell us about the rooms, window treatments and project requirements when you enquire.

Can I see samples before choosing?

Visit our Mill Hill showroom to explore samples and discuss your ideas. Contact the team first to arrange a suitable time.

What should I bring to an initial enquiry?

Photographs, approximate window dimensions and fabric or style ideas are useful. For motorised blinds, include details of existing controls or home automation. Final measurements and specifications need to be checked before making or ordering.

How do I arrange a visit?

Call 020 8906 9900 or email info@mkiuk.com. The team will arrange a suitable time with you.

How much will my curtains or blinds cost?

The specification affects the cost, including dimensions, fabric, lining, hardware, motorisation and fitting. Contact us with your requirements to discuss your project.
